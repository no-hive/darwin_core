Darwin Core Protocol is a hub with everything you need for your scientific findings on living organisms to reach the global scientific community, wherever you are. It is a decentralized way to collect data in the open Darwin Core standard, built for scientists, hobbyists and local communities.

It is a **Zero-asset Nonprofit DeSci** project: there is no token, no fees and nothing to buy. It is made for scientists who want less bureaucracy in their work, not only for people who already know Web3.
