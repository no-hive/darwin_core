This is where a person becomes part of the community, and where they gain power.

<Note>
Of the ideas on this page, shared findings and small groups belong to the current alpha. Crowdfunding and retrofunding are planned, and so are the invitation to a matching group and the welcome voting right, which come with the app rework. See [Project State](/project-state).
</Note>

## Start with an invitation

The first thing a new person gets is an invitation to the group that best matches their interests. Groups are kept as simple as possible. Just by showing interest you join the community and receive a welcome voting right.

## Come together around shared findings

Several scientists can be attached to a single finding. If you made a discovery as a group, it is a shared finding.

## Honest crowdfunding and retrofunding

Receive honest crowdfunding and honest retrofunding. If 1,000 arrives for your group, you split it according to how your findings turned out.

## Form small communities

A community can be a group of scientists or something larger. You control your findings and keep power over your own domain. Groups can split apart and join together, the way LiquidDAO organizations do, and communities can be connected to each other: see [LiquidDAO](/concept/liquid-dao).

## From local power to global power

Through your small piece of work you gain a say over the protocol as a whole, and you can move it forward.
