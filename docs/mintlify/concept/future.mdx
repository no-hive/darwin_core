This page is about long-term plans and where all of this can lead if the community and its power are well distributed. We see the project not just as a layer of decentralized data, but as a **layer of data verification**.

<Note>
Everything on this page is planned. None of it is part of the current alpha. See [Project State](/project-state).
</Note>

## Your small step can unlock humanity's biggest secrets

Thousands of secrets on Earth are still undiscovered, and we can look wider at how we coordinate. Archaeological cities lie hidden under jungles, and the seabed is barely explored. The cultural and historical layer holds tens of thousands more local secrets that we cannot uncover without you.

## Darwin Core is more than recording findings

We believe that, with the rise of AI and robotics, the future of exploring our planet belongs to local hobbyists. Nobody but us will study our home, and it is the one step we must take before setting out on a journey through the galaxy: we must know who we are and where we live.

In 5 to 7 years we see huge numbers of robots and models studying Earth, run not by corporations but by you and your supporters, each responsible for a local piece and a local expertise. Taking responsibility for your small piece is what will truly reveal the secrets that are out there.

We do not expect one big corporation to do this. It makes little sense, and every ecosystem has its own particularities. We expect the next 5 to 7 years to bring a boom of hobby robotics for studying the world, and attaching sensors to a robot is easy, which will give observational science a huge boost.

## Physical proof

We connect to hardware so that every observation can be confirmed with zero-knowledge proofs, like a black box on a physical module. It proves that the observation really exists, was not generated, and happened at a specific place and time. This takes data verification to a new level.

## Global coordination

The second layer of trust is people. Through the community, people coordinate and confirm each other's data, creating a global organization of verification.

## What awaits us together depends only on you

Our nearest milestone is to strengthen the community enough for our research to reach describing the giant squids that live in Antarctica. We know they exist, but we do not know what they are. It is only one of thousands of riddles still ahead.
