Darwin Core Protocol is a hub with everything you need for your scientific findings on animals, plants, fungi, bacteria and living organisms in general to reach the global scientific community, wherever you are. Free tools, a single decentralized registry of findings, privacy and a public profile live in one place.

## What is Darwin Core

Darwin Core is the open standard scientists use to describe observations of living organisms: what was found, where and when. We are building a decentralized way to collect that data.

## Free tools

All our tools are free, and they are there to make working with your data easier. Start locally, with tools you can use right in your own tables for everyday work. When you are ready, move up to the tools that connect you to a single worldwide registry of findings, a decentralized one where anyone can upload anything.

See [Free tools](/technical/free-tools) for the list.

## The path we want to give you

With this platform you can record an observation and, quickly, with proof and without any other tools, carry it to the point where it is used in your scientific work. You may not even notice that along the way you gain real power in the project: see [Community](/concept/community).
