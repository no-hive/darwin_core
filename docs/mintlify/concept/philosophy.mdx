## Why this is a non-profit

Decentralized science works the way any science works: an action or an innovation brings real benefit only after a very long time. That is typical for a non-profit, and it is exactly why Darwin Core Protocol is a non-profit and not a business. The benefit comes very late, almost nobody is ready to fund it, and yet somebody has to do it.

## Zero-asset: no token, ever

Darwin Core Protocol has no token and never will. A token would add speculation to a project whose benefit comes slowly. This is the meaning of **zero-asset non-profit**.

## No temporary gods

Crypto projects are often positioned the same way: look complex and unclear, create a temporary god to worship, and invite people to put money into it by buying its token. Our path is different. We have no token, and any paragraph that a person outside Web3 cannot fully understand is a sign that we are going the wrong way.

## Who it is for

Our audience is scientists who want to make their lives easier by removing as much bureaucracy as possible, the bureaucracy that stops them from reaching a new level, locally or internationally. They do not have to care about Web3.

## What we are trying to change

In decentralized science, projects lean heavily on a **social layer of coordination**, and at the same time there is no team behind them except a single creator. This is the combination we work with.

Because of that, the project relies as much as possible on:

- **Open source code.**
- **No fees.**
- **Free resources.**
- **Traffic from SEO**, which means zero marketing.
- **Traffic from contributing authors** who are happy to share the project for free (see [For authors](/contribute/authors)).

## A foundation, not a launch

The project is built not to shoot up but to grow gradually, the way a forest comes back after a fire. **Sustainable non-profit** is a good name for this approach.

The project is the base of a living ecosystem that will not die in a short period of time. If the community comes to like it in two years, it will be alive in two years. If they come to like it in three, it will be alive in three.

This is not an attempt to start a community in a short time. It is the creation of a foundation that can take off at any moment.

## Findings are a common heritage

A finding should not disappear into a private archive. Findings are a **common heritage**, and the person who made one deserves a public record of it and a path for it to reach science.

## Everyone studies their own home

Nobody but us will study our home. We believe the future of studying the planet belongs to **local hobbyists equipped with AI and robotics**, each taking responsibility for a local piece of the world. See [Future](/concept/future).
