The protocol is responsible for **recording findings** in the Darwin Core standard. It is a separate element from the [voting stack](/technical/voting-stack); the two are connected through a [hook](/technical/hook).

## Real and non-real findings

While privacy is not connected, the upload form has a **"Not a real finding"** option for trying the protocol. Such findings are written into a separate group and journal, marked as not real.
