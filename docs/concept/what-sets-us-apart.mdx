Darwin Core Protocol is a hub where findings about living organisms reach the global scientific community. It is made for people who observe the natural world, from professional scientists to local hobbyists, and for the groups they form around their findings. See [What is Darwin Core Protocol](/concept/what-is-darwin-core).

It is a DeSci project with a serious focus on four things. The first three set us apart from everyone else.

## Who it is for

Our audience is not scientists who happen to be interested in Web3. It is scientists who want a lighter life: less of the bureaucracy that keeps them from reaching the next level, locally or internationally. That is why we hold ourselves to one rule in our own texts: if a paragraph is not fully clear to a person outside Web3, we are going in the wrong direction.

## 1. Solving real problems

We start from real problems that people who work with findings face today: observations that stay in notebooks and spreadsheets, findings that gather dust in archives, and the long, unproven road from a sighting to a scientific paper. Every part of the protocol exists because it answers one of these problems.

## 2. A serious social layer

Science is done by people. Darwin Core Protocol is built around shared findings, small communities and a path from local control to a say over the protocol itself. We are making a serious bid to build the social layer, so that people who contribute gain real power instead of just a profile. See [Community](/concept/community).

## 3. Innovation in coordination

Coordination is very important, and we are changing the whole approach to it, including the user experience. We are not only reworking blockchain UX. We are reworking how people see coordination itself, through two elements.

**A feed of recommendations instead of old voting interfaces.** Rather than a list of proposals to search through, people see a feed that surfaces the places where their coordination is needed. In the future we want to develop it so that you open the app and the system shows you the place where your voice matters most. The system wins, and people win, because they see their own power.

**The [LiquidDAO](/concept/liquid-dao) concept.** A system that solves many problems of coordination.

## 4. Abstracting users from the blockchain

We use the most modern technology available, but you should not have to think about it. Wallets and chains stay out of your way: you see people, groups and findings, not addresses and transactions. This overlaps with point 3, but while point 3 is about coordination, this one is about everything else.
