---
title: "Public profile"
description: "A profile that is truly yours"
---

A beautiful public profile makes you part of the community and turns your findings into something that is really yours, not something gathering dust in archives.

Your profile shows your name, your occupation, optional age (month and year only), interest tags, a feed of recent activity (votes in the DAO, contributions to the protocol, findings, checks and reviews) and a radar chart of your skills with an automatically chosen profile caption.
