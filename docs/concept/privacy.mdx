---
title: "Privacy"
description: "Hiding data without losing it"
---

A registry is not only about publicity. Hide what needs to stay hidden, and never lose a single record.

Some findings are sensitive, for example the location of a rare species. Privacy in Darwin Core Protocol means being able to **hide data without losing it**: the finding still exists in the registry and can still count, while its sensitive details stay hidden. Private data is part of the concept from the very beginning.

<Warning>
Privacy is **not connected yet** in the current alpha. Upload only findings that are definitely not sensitive. See [Project State](/project-state).
</Warning>
