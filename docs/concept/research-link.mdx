---
title: "From observation to publication"
description: "Connecting findings to scientific publications"
---

Record an observation and carry it into your scientific work quickly and with proof, without any other tools.

Darwin Core Protocol is building an **evidence layer** that helps you insert your findings into your research and confirm them, possibly right from the platform. The goal is a single place where an observation can travel from the field to a publication, with every step checkable.

<Note>
Connection to research is planned and **not part of the current alpha**. See [Project State](/project-state).
</Note>
