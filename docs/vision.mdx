Darwin Core Protocol is a project for recording observations of living organisms in the open Darwin Core standard and helping those findings reach the scientific community. It is designed for people who study the natural world, from professional scientists to local observers.

The current alpha is an early experiment. The wider community, research and hardware ideas on this page describe the project's direction, not features that are all available today. See [Project State](/project-state) for what exists now.

## For people who study the natural world

Science should not require people to navigate unnecessary bureaucracy or learn Web3 before they can contribute. The project aims to make it easier to record observations, work with data and carry findings into scientific work. Free tools are part of that goal, from tools for local tables to tools connected to the registry.

## A sustainable, zero-asset nonprofit

Darwin Core Protocol is a nonprofit project with no token, fees or paid offers. It is intended to grow gradually as an open foundation for research and community work, rather than depend on speculative assets or promises of quick growth.

The project is built around open source code, free resources and long-term work. Its purpose is to help useful findings reach science, even when that benefit takes time.

## Shared findings and small communities

Observations made together should be shareable as group findings. Communities can form around shared interests and local knowledge, while retaining control over their own work. The long-term direction is for groups to connect, cooperate and have a meaningful say in how the protocol develops.

Ideas such as matching new people with relevant groups, welcome voting rights, crowdfunding and retrofunding are planned; they are not all part of the current alpha.

## Coordination that recognizes contributions

The project aims to make participation easier to understand: instead of asking people to search through long lists of proposals, the app could surface where their input is useful. People who contribute findings and effort should gain a path to influence the shared system, not just receive a profile. The [LiquidDAO concept](/concept/liquid-dao) explores ways for organizations to coordinate while keeping the ability to evolve independently.

## From observation to research

The long-term goal is an evidence layer that helps people carry an observation from the field into research and make each step easier to verify. Connecting findings directly to research publications is planned and is not part of the current alpha.

## Local knowledge, global verification

The project sees a future where local scientists and hobbyists use AI, robotics and sensors to study the places they know. Communities could then help verify observations across regions. Hardware-based evidence, including zero-knowledge proofs, is a research direction, not a current feature.

A public profile is intended to connect people with their findings and contributions, giving their work a visible place in the community.
