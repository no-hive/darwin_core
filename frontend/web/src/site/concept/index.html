What is Darwin Core Protocol?

Still curled into a spiral, a new frond catches the light. In its unhurried unfolding, a whole forest begins.

context

An observation is never only a name. Place, time, method, and the people behind it give a record its meaning.

Where light
meets shadow

A soft beam brushes the moss. A frog pauses for a moment, and the forest seems to grow a little quieter.

Make records
legible

Darwin Core gives biodiversity data a shared structure, so one careful observation can be understood beyond its original home.

A living
protocol

The goal is not identical datasets. It is a way for independent collections and people to understand one another.
